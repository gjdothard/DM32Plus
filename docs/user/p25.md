# Monitoring P25

Select a stored P25 channel programmed with the Codeplug Editor. Keep
**Receive Only** enabled when you want passive monitoring. Receive filters
never enable transmission. The assigned-group picker also changes the transmit
target; Recent-call Hold and favorites leave it alone.

Dual Watch is unavailable when either channel uses P25, conventional or trunked. The radio explains the restriction
when you try to enable it. A saved watch containing P25 does not resume. Changing to a P25 channel ends Dual Watch.

In CPS, the channel's **Talkgroups** tab uses the same saved group contacts,
Add, Create and assign, ordering and removal controls as DMR. Assign IDs in
decimal, from 1 through 65535. The first assigned group is the initial transmit
target. P25 ignores contact timeslot overrides. Write the codeplug after saving.

On Home, turn the channel knob to select the next or previous configured P25
talkgroup, just as on DMR. It wraps at the ends and changes the receive hold
and transmit target together. The selected group and name appear on Home.
Up/Down still change channels. With no configured groups, the knob changes
channels as usual. Turning it while PTT is held or transmitting does not change
the group. Receive Only and system listen-only settings remain authoritative.

Saved contacts have a **Protocol** field: **DMR**, **P25**, or **DMR and P25**.
The channel editor lists contacts for its protocol. Creating a group inside a
channel sets that protocol automatically. Existing P25 assignments are
recognized when reading an older codeplug. Names and IDs can be different
between protocols, including two groups with the same numeric ID.

Press **\***, then **4 Talk to**, to select one of the channel's assigned
groups. **OK** holds reception on that group and selects it as the transmit
target; it does not transmit or disable Receive Only. Selecting a group briefly
reacquires the trunked control channel. The selection lasts until the channel or
codeplug reloads. **Programmed groups** resumes reception of the assigned list
without changing the selected transmit target. **All talkgroups** hears all
supported groups. Turn off CPS **Follow every group of the system** when the
channel should start with its programmed list rather than all groups.

Home shows the talkgroup ID and its programmed contact name, when available.
Unknown groups still show their number. A source radio ID appears when
decoded. **Last heard** means a remembered call, not one currently playing.
**Tuned** is the actual receiver frequency; it changes as trunking follows
voice assignments. **Control** is the acquired control channel.
Control and voice receive timeouts start after retuning completes, allowing
the new frequency its full reception window before the radio searches again.

From P25 Home, press **#** (the Context action) to open **Recent calls**.
The normal **OK** main menu remains available. The **Quick** action opens
**Quick Actions**. On a key map without a
dedicated Quick action, hold **OK** instead.

| Key | Action | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Power | Change transmit power when TX is permitted |
| 2 | Listen | Choose Programmed groups, All talkgroups or Favorites only; reception only |
| 3 | Hold | Hold the newest received group; press again to release to Programmed groups |
| 4 | Talk to | Choose an assigned group for both reception and the selected TX target |
| 5 | Recent | Browse recent calls; OK holds reception and # manages favorites |
| 6 | Favorites | Browse and manage this system's saved receive groups |
| 7 | System | Inspect status, decoded identity and signal |
| 8 | Details | Inspect tuned frequency, decode counters and the last skipped-call reason |
| 9 | Bright | Adjust screen brightness |

Listen and Hold leave the selected TX target unchanged. Talk to changes both
receive selection and that target; it never grants permission to transmit.
RX-only trunked channels keep their receive controls. Conventional channels show
**Trunked only** on Listen, Hold and Favorites. Their assigned-group picker and
diagnostics remain available. Unavailable actions explain why when selected.
No recent group means there is nothing to hold; save a group from Recent before
choosing Favorites only. Back leaves the Listen picker without applying a change.
Small displays offer the same numbered actions in a scrolling list.

In Recent calls or Favorite list, **OK** holds the selected group and returns
Home. **#** adds or removes that group as a favorite. A star marks a favorite.
Each system has four favorite slots. Names come from programmed group contacts.

Favorites survive power cycles. Saving one briefly reloads the P25 owner and
clears recent history and any temporary Hold. Other listening selections also
reset to the channel's programmed behavior when you change channels or reload
the codeplug. A filter change during a voice call takes effect on subsequent
grants; the current call finishes normally. Use **3 Hold** again, or choose **Programmed groups** or **All talkgroups**
in Listen, to release a Hold.

System identity is displayed only after it is decoded. `--` means unknown.
In CPS, open the trunked system's **Sites** table and enter an optional
**Site name** (up to 16 printable Latin-1 characters). Enter its broadcast
RFSS and site IDs to confirm the name. The site group number links the
system's control channels to that entry; it is separate from the broadcast ID.

Trunked Home shows the acquired site's name and keeps it while following a
voice call. Unnamed sites show RFSS/site numbers. **Candidate** identifies a
programmed site that is still being acquired. **Site mismatch** means the
decoded identity conflicts with the programmed assignments. Check the system,
site IDs and control frequencies in CPS. System details show the full site
name, decoded identity and exact signal strength.

On card Home, linked squares indicate acquired control, the speaker indicates
a voice grant or reception, and the search symbol indicates acquisition.
The adjacent bars show signal strength. RX-only permission stays visible.
In System details, **#** switches between identity and decoding details.
Status values use compact wording on smaller screens. The Talkgroups page
shows the channel name above its assignments.
**Last skip** can report encryption, an unsupported Phase 2 voice grant, or a
missing channel identifier plan. It describes the last refusal, not necessarily
the current call. These controls do not add decoding support for those calls.

Conventional P25 displays call information without trunking selection controls.
Available builds and board capabilities are in the
[feature matrix](../feature-matrix.md).
